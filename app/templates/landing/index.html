The problem

Spreadsheets don't run a solar company

📦

Equipment conflicts

Equipment reserved for one job shows up on another site.

⚡

Installation gaps

Installations marked "done" but warranties and stock never update.

💰

Finance disconnect

Finance chases payments with no link to the install or service record.

The solution

One system from customer to payment

Every financial change and status update is tied to a staff account and logged for review.

1

Customers & sites

Individuals, businesses, and government customers mapped to physical locations across Zambia.

2

Equipment & warehouse

Types, serials, and status rules from Available → Installed.

3

Installations & service

Schedule, assign technicians, complete installs, and log maintenance.

4

Finance & oversight

Invoices, payments, reports, and read-only auditor access.

How it works

Up and running in three steps

1

Set up the company

Admin creates staff accounts and assigns roles — Sales Rep, Operations Manager, Finance Officer, and more.

2

Register customers, sites & equipment

Warehouse loads stock. Operations schedules installations and assigns field technicians.

3

Run operations daily

Install, log service, invoice, collect payment, and run reports — all linked to the same records.

Staff are login users with roles. Technicians are field profiles. A person can exist in both.

Administrator Sales Rep Operations Manager Technician Warehouse Clerk Customer Service Finance Officer Auditor
